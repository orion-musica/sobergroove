# Sober Groove user guide

Version 0.1.0-alpha · M-VAVE FM-1 firmware

Sober Groove is a small companion that lives in your FM-1. A musician who lost the band and the flat moves onto your couch. You look after each other: you say "not today" every morning, ride out cravings together, and teach your buddy music until it plays the main stage.

> **Support, not treatment.** Sober Groove does not replace a doctor, a therapist or a support group. Stopping heavy drinking suddenly can be dangerous: talk to a GP before you stop. In a crisis in the UK or Ireland, Samaritans answer 24/7.

## 1. The panel at a glance

| Control | On the home screen | Elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **White keys** | Play your buddy's instrument | In a lesson: teach the notes |
| **Black keys 1–4** (from the left) | Care: meal, talk, move, sleep/wake | In SOS: the HALT answers |
| **Black key 5** | Start SOS | Starts SOS from almost every screen; hold 2 s during a wave to stop early |
| **HOME** (tap) | Status | Next page of status, then home |
| **HOME** (hold 1.5 s) | Say "not today" | Also from the status pages |
| **GLO** | Menu | Back. During SOS: Help, without stopping the wave |
| **SELECT knob** | | Move through choices |
| **SEL** | | Confirm |
| **SAVE** | Save now | Save now |
| **MASTER** | Volume | Volume |

The keys you can use right now are lit. FX, PLAY/STOP and the four effect knobs are not used in this version.

Every screen has a hint line at the bottom that says what the controls do there.

## 2. First start

The first time Sober Groove starts, it walks you through a short introduction. Each step continues with **SEL**.

1. **Splash.** "Stay off alcohol. One day at a time. For musicians in recovery." This screen shows for three seconds on every start.
2. **What is this?** A buddy who helps you stay sober: it counts sober days, helps ride cravings and learns music from you.
3. **Important.** Support, not treatment; see a GP before stopping heavy drinking.
4. **Choose your buddy.** Turn the knob and press SEL:
   - **Low**, bass: an FM bass on the white keys.
   - **Kit**, drums: kick, snare, hat and clap repeat across the white keys.
   - **Vinyl**, DJ: a short plucked stab.

   You can change the buddy later in Settings without losing anything.
5. **Prologue.** A knock at the door. Your buddy tells you what happened ("They kicked me out of the band. Then out of the flat."), asks "Can I crash here for a bit?" and you let them in. They sit on your couch: "You've got your own stuff too, I know. Same road. We'll figure it out."
6. **Help is here.** Where to look for help (see [section 11](#11-help-and-support-groups)).
7. **Ready.** Every morning, hold HOME and say "not today". Black key 5 is SOS.

## 3. The home screen

- **Top line:** the total of sober days (`DAY 12`) and the current stage (`STREET`).
- **The scene:** your buddy where the stage puts them: on your couch, under a street lamp, in a rehearsal room, on a club stage, on the main stage. A note bobs while they are awake; a moon and Zs show while they sleep.
- **Three meters** of five squares each:
  - **EN, energy:** rises with movement and sleep.
  - **MO, mood:** rises when you talk, say "not today", ride out a wave or teach.
  - **RH, rhythm:** rises with meals, movement and lessons.

  The meters drift down slowly while the buddy is awake (one point every three minutes) and never drop below 10 of 100. **Nothing dies and nothing is lost.** A low meter means the buddy would like some attention, nothing more.
- **Bottom line:** `HOME STATUS - KEY 5 SOS - GLO MENU`.

### Status

Tap **HOME** for status:

- **Page 1:** the total of sober days, large, because it never falls. Below it: the streak and best streak, waves ridden out, lessons and riffs.
- **Page 2 (tap HOME again):** what the next stage needs, as two bars: sober days and lessons.
- Tap HOME a third time to go home.

## 4. Saying "not today"

Each morning, **hold HOME for 1.5 seconds**. The buddy wakes up, a short fanfare plays and the screen says "Not today. Day 13."

- Your **total of sober days** goes up by one and never goes down.
- Your **streak** goes up by one. Your **best streak** follows it.
- If the new day completes a stage, the stage-up screen follows. Otherwise the buddy may share one thought (at most one a day).

The FM-1 has no clock that keeps time while it is switched off, so Sober Groove counts days like this:

- **While it stays on,** the next day opens 20 hours after the last "not today". Holding HOME before that shows "Today already counts. Keep going."
- **After switching on again,** the first hold asks "A new day since we last talked?" Choose **Yes, a new day** or **No, same day**. A day never counts twice: after "No", it waits 20 hours.

It is a promise to yourself, so the device trusts your answer.

## 5. Caring for your buddy

On the home screen the first four black keys are care actions. Caring for the buddy is also a small reminder to look after yourself.

| Key | Action | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Meal** | "Thanks for eating." Rhythm +20, mood +4. A bowl and a heart appear. |
| 2 | **Talk** | "I'm listening. How are you?" Pick 1–5 with the knob and press SEL. Mood rises either way (+8 for 4 or 5, +4 otherwise). |
| 3 | **Move** | "Nice move." Energy +12, rhythm +6. |
| 4 | **Sleep / wake** | "Good night." / "Good morning." While asleep, energy recovers one point every 15 seconds and the meters do not drift. |

## 6. SOS: riding out a craving

Press **black key 5** whenever a craving hits. SOS starts from the home screen, the status pages, the menu, lessons and most other screens.

A craving comes like a wave: it rises, peaks and passes. SOS gives you 20 minutes to ride it, with something for your breath and your hands.

1. **Start.** "I'm here. We'll ride it out." A calm bass note sounds at once. The white keys switch to a soft bell so you can play along.
2. **Breathing.** Every 10 seconds: **breathe in** for 5 seconds while the bass plays, **breathe out** for 5 seconds in silence. That is six breaths a minute.
3. **The wave.** A wave line grows towards the middle of the 20 minutes ("This is the peak.") and calms again ("It will pass."). A bar and a timer show how far you are.
4. **HALT check at 5 minutes.** "Quick check. Are you hungry, angry, lonely or tired?" Answer with black keys 1–4 or the knob. A tip follows:
   - **Hungry:** "Eat something now. Hunger can feel like a craving."
   - **Angry:** "Play it out. Hit the keys hard, then breathe." You can also **teach a riff** right there; the wave keeps time while you play.
   - **Lonely:** "Call someone now. Or text someone. GLO opens Help."
   - **Tired:** "Rest, don't drink. Lie down for ten minutes."

   The tip returns to the wave by itself after 15 seconds, or press SEL.
5. **Halfway (10 minutes).** "Halfway. Call someone?"
6. **Help at any time:** press **GLO**. The wave keeps time; GLO again returns to it.
7. **The end.** "Wave passed. You rode it out." A fanfare plays and **waves ridden** goes up by one.

**Stopping early:** hold **black key 5** for 2 seconds. "Feeling lighter?" Choose **Yes, exit** or **Not yet, back** (the default). Stopping early is fine: there is no penalty, and the buddy says "OK. I'm here if it comes back." Only a wave ridden to the end counts as a wave.

## 7. Lessons and the repertoire

You are your buddy's teacher. Every lesson is music you give them, and it shapes the artist they become.

### Teaching (GLO → Jam)

1. Open **GLO → JAM: TEACH ‹NAME›**.
2. **Play up to four notes** (Kit: four hits) on the white keys. Four boxes fill in as you play. After the fourth note the lesson continues by itself; with fewer, press **SEL**.
3. **"Got it. Let me try..."** Your buddy plays it back, one note at a time.
4. **The result:**
   - All right: **"I got it! New riff #N."** The riff joins the repertoire.
   - Not quite: **"3 of 4. Close!"** Notes the buddy got wrong show with a dashed edge. Choose **Teach again** (the buddy listens harder next time) or **Keep it like this** (your riff is saved as you played it: "It is our version now").

Either way, a finished lesson counts: **lessons +1**, rhythm +6 and mood +6.

How often the buddy slips up depends on the stage: about 1 note in 4 at LOST, 1 in 7 at STREET, 1 in 12 at GARAGE, 1 in 25 at CLUB and never on the MAIN STAGE. Each "Teach again" halves the chance, twice at most.

There is no obligation. If you do not teach, the buddy practises what it already knows; nothing is lost.

### The repertoire (GLO → Repertoire)

Every riff the buddy has learned, newest first: number, the sober day it was learned and its notes (Kit: the drum names). Turn the knob to choose and press **SEL** to hear it. The repertoire holds 48 riffs; after that the oldest makes room.

## 8. Stages and the story

A stage needs **both** a number of sober days **and** a number of lessons. A stage never falls, not even after a slip.

| Stage | Scene | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **LOST** | Slumped on your couch | the start |
| 2. **STREET** | Playing under a street lamp, a hat for coins | 3 sober days and 3 lessons |
| 3. **GARAGE** | Rehearsing with an amp | 14 days and 10 lessons |
| 4. **CLUB** | A small stage and a spotlight | 30 days and 20 lessons |
| 5. **MAIN STAGE** | Lights on the truss, a crowd with hands up | 90 days and 40 lessons |

A new stage shows an inverted screen with a fanfare and a short chapter of the story:

- **Street:** "Played on the street. Your riffs would not leave me alone."
- **Garage:** "Found a rehearsal room. Thinking about the old band sometimes."
- **Club:** "First sober gig. Thinking of texting our old drummer. Maybe."
- **Main stage:** "You let me in when I had nowhere to go. Thank you."

The buddy never moves out, not even after the main stage.

## 9. If there was a slip

**GLO → There was a slip.** The answer is set to **No, back** so it cannot be chosen by accident.

1. **"Thank you for telling me. A slip is not the end."**
2. **"What was it?"** Stress, people, a gig, being alone, other.
3. **"Who can help you today?"** Someone I trust, a helpline, my group, not sure yet.
4. **"Your progress stays."** Your total of sober days, waves, lessons and stage all stay. Only the current streak starts again. "Still on your couch."

Nothing is taken away from your buddy, and the story carries on.

## 10. The buddy's thoughts

Now and then, after the morning "not today", your buddy thinks out loud. At most once a day.

- Most thoughts are simple curiosity: "Cravings come like waves, I read. Funny. Good grooves do too."
- Some are about people and support groups: "Heard there are recovery groups for music people. Like a band, but for sober."
- Rarely, at most once a week, about a professional: "Some musicians have a producer for their head. A therapist. Sounds kind of interesting."

A thought shows **HMM** to close it. Thoughts about groups or a professional also show **MORE**, a short, calm explanation without phone numbers. Turn thoughts off in **Settings**.

## 11. Help and support groups

**GLO → Help** lists where to look. There are no phone numbers in the firmware: numbers change, so check them on the organisations' own websites.

- **Music Support** (UK): a free, confidential helpline (Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00) run by people from the music and live events industries, and recovery support groups.
- **Support groups** (press SEL): therapy groups led by a professional (ask your GP or local alcohol services), Music Support groups for music people, SMART Recovery (groups based on cognitive behavioural therapy) and AA, as one option among others.
- **Samaritans** (UK and Ireland): 24/7.
- **Your GP:** for a safe detox and referrals to individual or group therapy.
- **Someone you trust.**

## 13. Saving, power and time

- Sober Groove **saves by itself.** Important moments (a new day, a wave, a lesson, a slip, a setting) are saved within about a second, when no key is held. Smaller changes are saved at most once a minute.
- **SAVE** saves at once and shows "Saved.".
- Saves use two copies with a checksum, so a power cut during a save keeps the previous good copy.
- **Time switched off costs nothing.** The meters only drift while the buddy is awake and powered.
- Another firmware's save is ignored; Sober Groove then starts fresh.

## 14. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| "Today already counts" but it is a new day | The device stayed on for less than 20 hours. Switch it off and on: the first hold asks whether it is a new day. |
| No sound | Turn MASTER up; check Settings → Volume. |
| A key does nothing | Only lit keys do something on that screen. The hint line at the bottom lists them. |
| "Save failed. Will retry." | Let go of the keys; it retries by itself. If it keeps failing, note what you did and open an issue. |
| SOS does not start | It does not start during the first-start introduction or on the stage-up screen. Finish that screen first. |
| The buddy keeps sleeping | Press black key 4 to wake them. |

## 15. Questions

**Can my buddy die or leave?** No. Never.

**Does a slip reset everything?** No. Only the current streak restarts.

**Can I cheat the day counter?** You can, the same way you can lie to a diary. It is there to support you, not to judge you.

**Does it send anything anywhere?** No. Sober Groove never uses the radio, and nothing leaves the device.

**Is my data private?** It stays on your FM-1. To anyone else it looks like a synth.

**Can I go back to the normal FM-1 firmware?** Yes: install the M-VAVE firmware the same way you installed Sober Groove ([INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md)).
