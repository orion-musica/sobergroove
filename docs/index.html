THE STORY

A buddy with nowhere else to go.

You’ve got your own stuff too. That’s fine: you’re on the same road, a few steps further today. On a bad day, the roles turn.

01 · KNOCK KNOCK

“They kicked me out of the band. Then out of the flat.”

02 · COME IN

“Can I crash here for a bit?” They take the corner of your couch.

03 · TEACH

You play a riff. Your buddy listens, then tries it back.

04 · MAIN STAGE

“You let me in when I had nowhere to go.”

How it works

01

Not today.

Hold HOME each morning. The total of sober days only ever grows, and your streak and best streak follow.

02

Ride the wave.

Key 5 starts a 20-minute SOS: breathe in with the bass, out in silence, play along. A craving peaks and passes.

03

Teach your buddy.

Play up to four notes. Your buddy tries them back, slips up early on, then nails it. Every lesson joins its repertoire.

04

A slip is not the end.

The streak starts again. Days, waves, lessons and the stage all stay. Nothing dies, nothing is taken away.

SOS · BLACK KEY 5

When a craving hits,
press one key.

  • A 20-minute wave on screen: it rises, peaks and passes.
  • Six breaths a minute: in with the bass, out in silence.
  • At five minutes, a HALT check: hungry, angry, lonely, tired? A small tip for each.
  • Help is one press away, and the wave keeps time.
  • Stop whenever you like. No penalty, ever.

PEOPLE BEFORE THE DEVICE

A tiny buddy is not enough on its own.

Sober Groove is support, not treatment. Stopping heavy drinking suddenly can be dangerous: talk to a GP first. Check current numbers on each organisation’s own site.

Music Support

UK. A free, confidential helpline run by people from the music industry, Mon–Fri 9–17, and recovery support groups.

Therapy groups

Led by a professional, through the NHS or local alcohol services. Ask your GP.

SMART Recovery

Groups based on cognitive behavioural therapy, also online.

Samaritans

UK and Ireland, 24/7, for any moment that feels too heavy.

Your GP

For a safe detox and referrals to one-to-one or group therapy.

AA

One option among others.
